# 📖 The Ultimate ResearchMind User Guide

Welcome to ResearchMind, your personal AI research partner! This guide provides an in-depth look at all core features to help you become a ResearchMind expert user.

---

## 🗺️ Understanding the Main Interface

When you open ResearchMind, you'll see a clean layout:

- **Left Side (👈): Agent Selection Panel** - Your command center for switching between the three core AI agents.
- **Center (💬): Chat History & Interaction Panel** - Review your conversation history with the AI and issue new commands or upload files here.
- **Right Side (🔬): Results & Interaction Panel** - Generated reports, data, and interactive charts and 3D models are presented here.

## 🚀 Step 1: Choose Your AI Agent

In the Agent Selection Panel on the left, choose the best agent for your task:

1.  📚 **Literature Research Agent**
    - **Use it for:** Quickly understanding a research field, finding key papers, or generating a structured literature review.
    - **Core capability:** The AI reads papers, writes summaries, and clarifies research trends for you.

2.  📊 **Database Query Agent**
    - **Use it for:** Querying the crystal structure, band structure, density of states, or other physical properties of a material.
    - **Core capability:** The AI accurately retrieves material data for you from massive professional databases.

3.  🔬 **Simulation & Calculation Agent**
    - **Use it for:** Calculating physical properties from scratch (e.g., thermal conductivity, phonon spectrum) when you have a material's structure file (like a CIF).
    - **Core capability:** The AI runs complex simulations for you and visualizes the results.

## 🎮 In-Depth Guide to Core Features

### 📚 How to Master the [Literature Research Agent]

**Goal:** To generate an in-depth research report on the "stability of perovskite solar cells."

1.  **Select Agent:** Click on 📚 **Literature Research Agent** on the left.
2.  **Give a Specific Command:** In the chatbox, type your research topic. **The more specific the command, the better the result.**
    > "Generate a detailed report on the latest strategies for improving the stability of perovskite solar cells, focusing on material modification and encapsulation techniques."
3.  **Analyze and Use the Results:**
    - **Read the Report:** A structured report will be generated on the right, typically including an **introduction, summaries of key papers, discussion of technologies by category, and a conclusion**. You can copy the content directly.
    - **Download the Data:** Below the report, a **download link (⬇️) for a CSV file** will be provided. This file contains the **title, authors, journal, year, abstract, and URL** of all references, making it a powerful tool for managing your literature.
    - **Ask Follow-up Questions:** You can continue the conversation based on the report. For example: "Please elaborate on the ALD encapsulation technique mentioned in the report."

`[GIF: A screen recording showing the process from selecting the Literature Agent, to typing a specific command, generating the report, downloading the CSV, and asking a follow-up question.]`

### 📊 How to Master the [Database Query Agent]

**Goal:** To find the crystal structure and band gap of "Diamond."

1.  **Select Agent:** Click on 📊 **Database Query Agent** on the left.
2.  **Give Command:** Clearly state the material and properties you want to query in the chatbox.
    > "Find the crystal structure and band gap of diamond"
3.  **Analyze and Use the Results:**
    - **View the Data:** The right panel will display **detailed material information** from authoritative databases (like Materials Project), including lattice parameters, space group, band gap value, etc. There is usually a **copy button (📋)** next to the data for your convenience.
    - **Interact with the 3D Model:** You will see an **interactive 3D model of the crystal structure**.
        - **Rotate:** Hold down the left mouse button and drag.
        - **Zoom:** Scroll the mouse wheel.
        - **Pan:** Hold down the right mouse button and drag.
    - **Download Structure Files:** A **download link (⬇️) for formats like CIF/POSCAR** is usually available below the model, allowing you to import the structure into other software.

`[GIF: A screen recording showing a query for diamond, interacting with the 3D model (rotating/zooming), and clicking to copy the band gap data and download the CIF file.]`

### 🔬 How to Master the [Simulation & Calculation Agent]

**Goal:** To calculate the phonon spectrum of a `MoS2.cif` file.

1.  **Select Agent:** Click on 🔬 **Simulation & Calculation Agent** on the left.
2.  **Upload File:** Click the **"Upload File" button (📎)** below the chatbox and select your `MoS2.cif` file.
    `[Image: A screenshot highlighting the "Upload File" button.]`
3.  **Give Command:** After the file is uploaded, enter your calculation request in the chatbox.
    > "Calculate the phonon spectrum for this MoS2 structure and analyze its dynamical stability"
4.  **Analyze and Use the Results:**
    - **View Numerical Results:** After the calculation, the right panel will first display key **parameters and conclusions**, such as "No imaginary frequencies found, the structure is dynamically stable."
    - **Analyze the Chart:**
        - You will get a professional **phonon spectrum chart**. Hover your mouse over the plot to see the values of specific data points.
        - There is usually a **download button (⬇️)** in the top-right corner of the chart to save the image as a PNG or SVG file.
    - **Interact with the 3D Model:** The crystal structure you uploaded will also be displayed as a 3D model for your reference.

`[GIF: A screen recording showing the upload of a MoS2.cif file, entering the command, and then viewing the phonon spectrum, hovering for data, and downloading the chart.]`

## 💰 Understanding the Billing Policy

**ResearchMind's billing model is "Pay-Per-Use," designed for transparency and fairness.**

- **When is it free?**
  - General chatting with the AI, asking questions, or reviewing your chat history.
  - Using the Database Query Agent for routine material property lookups.

- **When will you be charged?**
  - Charges apply only when the AI needs to use **costly external tools** to fulfill your request. This primarily includes:
    1.  **Literature Research Agent:** Performing deep, multi-source literature searches and generating reports.
    2.  **Simulation & Calculation Agent:** Executing resource-intensive physical simulations (like thermal conductivity or phonon spectrum calculations) on cloud servers.

- **How does billing work?**
  - Before executing a command that may incur a fee, the AI will **typically ask for your confirmation**.
  - Every paid tool has a clear price. You can view the details on the "Billing Information" page within the application.

**The Core Idea:** Simple queries and interactions are free. You only pay when you use powerful, resource-heavy features.

## 💡 FAQ & Pro Tips

- **Q: My calculation failed / my query returned no results. What should I do?**
  - **A:** First, check your input. For calculations, ensure the uploaded **CIF file is valid**. For queries, try **using different keywords** (e.g., "Silicon Dioxide" instead of its chemical formula "SiO2"). If the problem persists, try simplifying your command.

- **Q: Can I save my work?**
  - **A:** Of course! All your **conversation history and generated results are automatically saved to your account**. You can review, copy, or download your past work at any time.

- **Pro Tip 1: The Art of the Command**
  - **Be specific, not vague.** Instead of "find a new material," say "find a transparent conducting material with a high dielectric constant and a wide band gap."

- **Pro Tip 2: Use Follow-up Questions**
  - The AI remembers the context of the conversation. You can ask **multiple follow-up questions** to delve deeper, just like discussing with a real expert.

- **Pro Tip 3: Reuse Your Results**
  - You can download a CIF file from the [Database Query Agent] and directly upload it to the [Simulation & Calculation Agent] for a seamless workflow!
